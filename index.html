/ About Us / Quality Education
Our commitment

Quality Education

Learning that builds knowledge, confidence, practical skills and a strong foundation for the future.

Helping every learner progress

At Raheena Jibreel Memorial School, quality education means helping learners develop the knowledge and skills they need to understand the world around them. We value purposeful teaching, steady progress, curiosity and a classroom culture where learners are encouraged to ask questions and take part in their learning.

Our educational approach supports academic development while recognising the importance of discipline, good conduct and personal responsibility. We aim to help learners strengthen their reading, writing, numeracy, communication and problem-solving skills as they progress through their education.

Our educational priorities

  • Build a sound foundation in key academic subjects.
  • Encourage active participation, independent thinking and curiosity.
  • Support consistent learning habits, revision and personal effort.
  • Promote respect, responsibility and cooperation in the classroom.
  • Connect knowledge with positive character and everyday life.

A foundation for lifelong learning

We believe learning is an ongoing journey. By encouraging perseverance and a positive attitude toward learning, we aim to prepare pupils to approach future educational opportunities with confidence and integrity.

Our promise

To encourage purposeful learning, personal growth and a respectful attitude toward knowledge.

/ About Us / Islamic Values
Character and faith

Islamic Values

Supporting learning with faith, good manners, honesty, compassion and responsibility.

Knowledge with morals

Islamic values are an important part of the school's educational identity. Learning is not only about acquiring information; it is also an opportunity to develop integrity, kindness, patience and a sense of responsibility toward others.

Through Islamic studies and the daily expectations of school life, learners are encouraged to appreciate good manners, speak respectfully, act honestly, care for others and understand the importance of fulfilling their responsibilities. These values can guide pupils in their relationships with family, classmates, teachers and the wider community.

Values we encourage

  • Honesty: being truthful and acting with integrity.
  • Respect: treating teachers, classmates and community members with consideration.
  • Kindness: showing compassion and helping others when possible.
  • Discipline: developing self-control and taking responsibility for actions.
  • Faith and learning: valuing Islamic knowledge alongside the pursuit of beneficial education.

Our motto, “Knowledge with Morals,” reflects the belief that education should help learners become not only more knowledgeable, but also more thoughtful and responsible.

Our motto

Knowledge with Morals

Encouraging learning that is guided by character, faith and respect.

/ About Us / Safe Environment
A place to learn and grow

Safe Environment

A welcoming, orderly and supportive school atmosphere helps learners focus, participate and develop confidence.

Helping learners feel ready to learn

A conducive learning environment supports concentration, confidence and positive relationships. At Raheena Jibreel Memorial School, we value a school atmosphere where pupils are treated with dignity, encouraged to participate and guided to show respect for others.

Clear expectations, orderly routines and constructive communication can help learners understand their responsibilities and feel more comfortable asking questions or seeking guidance. A positive environment also depends on cooperation among pupils, educators and families.

What a supportive environment encourages

  • Respectful interactions among pupils and staff.
  • Good classroom routines and responsible behaviour.
  • Clean, organised spaces that support learning.
  • Open communication when a learner needs help or guidance.
  • Cooperation, inclusion and care for school property.

We encourage families to work in partnership with the school so that pupils receive consistent support for their learning, conduct and wellbeing.

Shared responsibility

A positive school environment grows when pupils, staff and families work together with respect and care.

Our programmes

Conventional Section

Building strong academic foundations, essential skills and positive learning habits.

Academic learning for a strong foundation

The Conventional Section focuses on academic learning and the development of essential skills that support pupils throughout their education. A strong foundation in core subjects helps learners communicate clearly, work with numbers, think critically and understand new ideas.

Alongside subject knowledge, learners benefit from regular practice, active participation, reading, discussion and opportunities to apply what they have learned. Good study habits and a sense of responsibility are also important parts of educational progress.

Learning priorities

  • Develop literacy, numeracy and communication skills.
  • Strengthen understanding of core academic subjects.
  • Encourage curiosity, reasoning and problem-solving.
  • Build effective study habits, revision routines and perseverance.
  • Promote teamwork, respectful discussion and personal responsibility.

Preparing for the next stage

Our aim is to help learners gain the confidence and academic preparation needed to continue learning, take on new challenges and make thoughtful decisions as they grow.

Who is this for?

This section is intended for learners following the school's conventional academic pathway. Please contact the school for current class levels and admission details.

Thafeez Section

Supporting Qur'an memorisation through consistency, revision, recitation and guidance.

A focused journey of Qur'an memorisation

The Thafeez Section is centred on memorising the Holy Qur'an and developing a steady, respectful approach to recitation and revision. Memorisation is a gradual process that calls for patience, consistency, attention and regular practice.

Learners benefit from a structured routine, guidance from their instructors and time to revise previously memorised passages. Revision is especially important because it helps learners strengthen retention while continuing to progress at a manageable pace.

Key areas of focus

  • Qur'an memorisation supported by regular revision.
  • Careful recitation and attention to pronunciation.
  • Patience, discipline and consistent learning routines.
  • Respect for the Qur'an and the learning process.
  • Encouragement and guidance as learners progress.

Partnership with families

Families can support learners by encouraging regular revision, maintaining a consistent routine and celebrating steady effort. Contact the school for details about current schedules and section requirements.

Learning through consistency

Small, regular efforts and thoughtful revision are important parts of a sustainable memorisation journey.

Primary Islamic Section

Introducing young learners to foundational Islamic knowledge, worship, manners and values.

Building a foundation in Islamic learning

The Primary Islamic Section is designed around the foundational stage of Islamic education. It provides an opportunity for young learners to become familiar with essential Islamic teachings and develop an understanding of the values that guide everyday conduct.

At this stage, clear explanations, repetition, guided practice and age-appropriate learning activities can help pupils build confidence and retain what they learn. Lessons can also encourage pupils to ask questions and connect their learning with respectful behaviour at home and at school.

Areas of learning

  • Foundational Islamic knowledge and teachings.
  • Introduction to Qur'anic learning and recitation as taught by the school.
  • Good manners, respect and personal responsibility.
  • Understanding the importance of worship and everyday conduct.
  • Developing positive habits through practice and revision.

The goal is to nurture a strong beginning in Islamic education and encourage learners to continue developing their knowledge and character.

Foundations first

Young learners benefit from clear guidance, steady practice and encouragement as they build their understanding.

Secondary Islamic Section

Encouraging deeper understanding, thoughtful reflection and responsibility through continued Islamic education.

Continuing the journey of Islamic knowledge

The Secondary Islamic Section supports learners as they continue developing their Islamic knowledge and understanding. As pupils mature, they can be encouraged to engage more thoughtfully with what they study, ask considered questions and recognise how knowledge can inform personal conduct and community relationships.

Continued study can strengthen understanding of Islamic teachings, encourage responsible decision-making and reinforce the importance of integrity, compassion, discipline and respect.

Learning priorities

  • Deepen understanding of Islamic teachings and principles.
  • Encourage reflection, discussion and responsible questions.
  • Reinforce good manners, honesty and accountability.
  • Develop independent study and revision habits.
  • Connect learning with respectful conduct and community responsibility.

For information about specific subjects, class levels, timetables and admission requirements, please contact the school directly.

Growing in understanding

Continued learning gives pupils opportunities to build knowledge, strengthen character and take greater responsibility for their choices.

Ummahat Section

A dedicated learning pathway for Ummahat, centred on beneficial knowledge and continued learning.

Making space for continued learning

The Ummahat Section provides a dedicated pathway for Ummahat to engage in learning within a setting shaped by the school's educational and Islamic values. Continued learning can help participants deepen their knowledge, reflect on what they study and strengthen their connection to beneficial education.

A supportive learning atmosphere values respect, consistency, shared learning and the different responsibilities participants may balance in their daily lives. The specific content, timetable and format of this section should be confirmed directly with the school.

Our learning approach

  • Encourage continued pursuit of beneficial knowledge.
  • Promote respectful discussion and shared learning.
  • Support consistency, reflection and revision.
  • Keep good character and responsibility central to learning.
  • Provide a welcoming atmosphere for participants.

For current programme details, schedules and admission information, please contact the school using the contact details on the homepage.
